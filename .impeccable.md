## Design Context

### Users
Developer/designer hiring managers, recruiters, and peers visiting Luke Climenhage's personal portfolio to evaluate his work and background.

### Brand Personality
Minimal, clean, considered. Every element earns its place. No decorative noise.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark theme centered on deep purple (`#1a0b2e`). Purple glowing accents (`rgba(147, 51, 234, ...)`). White text hierarchy on dark surfaces. The interactive canvas grid is the signature visual — motion and glow are reserved for it, not scattered across the UI.

### Design Principles
1. Restraint over decoration — if it doesn't serve the content, remove it
2. Consistency in hierarchy — white for primary, gray-400 for secondary, never mixed
3. Motion belongs to the canvas — avoid competing animations in components
4. Inline styles are never acceptable — Tailwind utilities or scoped `<style>` only
5. Decorative images use `alt=""` — meaningful images use descriptive alt text
